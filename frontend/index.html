FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Clear, honest answers about smart contract immutability, blind voting mechanics, gas, and privacy on Ethereum Sepolia.

Each poll maintains an internal mapping hasVoted[pollId][voterAddress] directly in the MultiPollVoting.sol smart contract on Ethereum Sepolia. Once your vote transaction is mined, that slot flips to true. Any further vote call from the same address on that pollId automatically reverts at the EVM bytecode level.

When a poll creator enables Blind Voting, vote tallies and option percentages are hidden from frontend views while the ballot is active. This eliminates herd mentality and social proof bias. Once the scheduled duration ends or the creator concludes the poll, the full tallies are revealed transparently.

No. VOICE is deployed on Ethereum Sepolia (Chain ID 11155111), an official Ethereum testnet. Gas fees are paid in free Sepolia test ETH, which you can claim instantly from free public faucets such as Google Cloud Web3 Faucet or Sepolia PoW Faucet.

Cancelling (cancelPoll) halts an active poll immediately before its scheduled end time; it can only be performed by the creator. Archiving (archivePoll) hides ended or cancelled polls from discovery lists. Archiving does not delete blockchain history: all votes and receipts remain permanently verifiable on Etherscan.

No. The contract is non-custodial and contains no admin overrides or backdoors. Every vote is signed by your own wallet and recorded immutably in Ethereum block history. Neither the creator nor protocol developers can modify or remove your cast vote.

Every vote transaction emits a VoteCast(pollId, voter, optionIndex) event logged in the block receipt. You can inspect your transaction hash on Sepolia Etherscan or paste it into our dedicated Vote Verifier to decode the parameters with cryptographic proof.
