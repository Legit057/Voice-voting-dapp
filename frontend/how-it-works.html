ARCHITECTURE & PROTOCOL

HOW VOICE WORKS

VOICE combines gas-efficient Ethereum smart contracts with cryptographic blind ballots and decentralized storage for community consensus.

1

Decentralized Ballot Launch

Any user can call createPoll(...) on the Sepolia smart contract. The creator specifies between 2 and 20 options, a duration from 1 minute up to 30 days, and chooses whether to enable Blind Voting.

2

One-Address One-Vote Enforcement

Voters connect their Web3 wallet and invoke vote(pollId, optionId). The contract updates the internal tally and permanently locks hasVoted[pollId][voter] = true, mathematically eliminating double voting and Sybil manipulation.

3

Mathematical Reveal & Resolution

In standard ballots, results update live with percentage progress bars. In Blind ballots, vote tallies are hidden until block.timestamp >= endTime. Once concluded, the winning option receives the official winner stamp or ties are reported.
